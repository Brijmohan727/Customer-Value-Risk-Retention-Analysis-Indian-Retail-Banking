# 💰 Customer Value, Risk & Retention Analysis – Indian Retail Banking
 
A data analytics project that identifies which customers are about to leave, which ones are sleeping, and which ones need help before they default.
 
**10,000 customers | ₹2.5 billion | 7 dashboards | Real-world problem**
 
---
 
## 🎯 The Problem
 
Your bank has customers scattered across 10 cities. You know their credit score, their balance, their transactions. But you don't know:
 
❌ **Who's going to leave?** (high-risk customers)  
❌ **Where's the money at risk?** (which segments)  
❌ **Who hasn't logged in for months?** (dormant accounts)  
❌ **Who's bleeding money?** (negative cash flow)  
 
Every customer gets treated the same. That's the problem.

## ✨ The Cool Part
 
This isn't just dashboards. Every number has a story:
 
- **"Why ₹203.9M at risk?"** → Because these 642 customers are high-balance AND high-risk (credit score + negative cash flow)
- **"Why focus on dormant?"** → RBI says dormant = compliance issue. Plus ₹724M locked up.
- **"Why cross-sell?"** → Customers with 2+ products stay longer. 2,013 have only 1.
- **"Why monitor 3,892?"** → They're inactive (12-24 months no transaction). One more month and they're dormant.
**Data flows to action. That's the difference.**
